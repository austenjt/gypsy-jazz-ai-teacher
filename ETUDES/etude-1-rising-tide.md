# Etude 1: Rising Tide

Chart: *Minor Swing* (Reinhardt-Grapelli), A minor, the 16-bar A section as written in the iReal chart: Am6 Am6 Dm6 Dm6 / E7 E7 Am6 Am6 / Dm6 Dm6 Am6 Am6 / Bb7 E7 Am6 E7, here played with the chart's alternate chords in bars 9-13 (Dm7, G13b9, Cmaj7, Fmaj7) and the b9 colour on E7 in bar 6. See `SONGS/minor-swing.md`.

Standard tuning `E A D G B e`. Notes are named in written guitar pitch (the sounding pitch is an octave lower). 4/4, swung eighth notes: **quarter = 160** to learn it, work up to 180-200. Each bar has eight note slots (1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &) of three columns each, so two-digit frets fit. A slot with no fret is a rest. Chord symbols sit above each bar with the bar number.

## The idea

Pure arpeggio sweeps. Each pair of bars climbs the chord and comes back down, so the line breathes in and out over the two-bar chord. The voice-leading carries through the changes: the D at the end of E7 in bar 14 steps down to the C that opens the Am6 in bar 15. Bar 10 uses the b9 (Ab) over G13b9. The final bar runs the E7 arpeggio down to the low G#, which pulls straight back to A in the top of the form.

## Practice notes

- Start with only bars 1-2, slowly, with strict alternate picking.
- Hear the voice-leading: bar 14 ends on D4, bar 15 starts on C4 (7th of E7 down to the 3rd of Am6).
- Play it again but stop each bar on its first note to hear the chord roots move A, D, E, A, D, G, C, F, Bb, E, A, E.

Every pitch in the tab was checked against the chord in its bar by script. Fingerings were chosen to keep the hand moving as little as possible; they are one way to play it, not the only way. Passing notes marked in the idea section are the only notes outside the chord.
