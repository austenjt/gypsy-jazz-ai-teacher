# Etude 2: Side Steps

Chart: *Hungaria* (Django Reinhardt), key of G, the 32-bar form from the iReal chart: G6/9 G6/9 Ab6/9 Ab6/9 / G6/9 G6/9 E7 E7 (A), A7 A7 D7 D7 / G/B Bbo7 Am7 D7 (B), the A again, then Am7 Cm6 G/B Bbo7 / Am7 D7 G6/9 G6/9 (C). Ab6/9 is a passing chord a half step above the tonic, and Bbo7 is a dim7 passing chord. See `SONGS/hungaria.md`.

Standard tuning `E A D G B e`. Notes are named in written guitar pitch (the sounding pitch is an octave lower). 4/4, medium up swing, swung eighth notes: **quarter = 140** to learn it, work up to 160-180. Each bar has eight note slots of three columns each, so two-digit frets fit. A slot with no fret is a rest. Chord symbols sit above each bar with the bar number. The line under each row of tab labels every note by its role in that bar's chord: `R` root, `3` or `b3`, `5`, `b5`, `6`, `9`, `b7`, `bb7`, and `a` for a chromatic approach or enclosure note that is not in the chord. A repeated note repeats its label.

Written to the `guitar-etude` fingering rule: while a chord lasts, consecutive notes are at most 3 frets apart and on the same or an adjacent string; at a chord change the line moves at most 6 frets; each bar fits a four-fret window.

## The idea

Rhythmic and chromatic. Twenty-two bars open with an eighth-note rest and several bars have rests inside them or at the end, which gives the line the bounce of Django-style playing. Notes outside the chords are only **chromatic approach notes** (a semitone from the chord tone that follows) and **enclosures** (one note a semitone above the target, one below, then the target). Enclosures: Bb and Ab around A in bars 1 and 17, C and Bb around B in bars 5, 21 and 31, and Ab and F# around the final G in bar 32. Approach notes are in bars 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16, 19, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27, 29 and 30. Bars 2, 6 and 20 repeat a note on the offbeat (a **double note**). The Bbo7 bars (14 and 28) are plain dim7 arpeggios, the chromatic bass step between G/B and Am7. The last bar rests on the final eighth after the G.

## Practice notes

- The approach and enclosing notes should lean into the target: play them lightly and put the weight on the chord tone they lead to.
- Accent the beats with chord tones.
- Play bars 1, 5 and 17 until the enclosure is automatic: two notes either side of the target, then the arpeggio.
- Bars 3-4 are on Ab6/9, a half step above the tonic: watch where the approach notes land.

Every pitch in the tab was checked against the chord in its bar by script (`.agents/skills/guitar-etude/scripts/check_etude.py`), and so was the fingering rule: inside a chord the biggest jump between notes is 3 frets, at a chord change it is 2 frets, and no bar needs more than a four-fret window. Approach notes and enclosures were checked as sitting one semitone from the chord tone that follows them. The frets run from 0 to 7. The etude has not been played on an instrument; fingerings are one way to play it, not the only way.
