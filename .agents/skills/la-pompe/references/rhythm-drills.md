# Rhythm drills

Ordered roughly by difficulty. Each drill is 2–5 minutes. Do not run the whole list in one session.

## 1. The click

No chords. Pick one string, play quarter notes with rest strokes at 60 bpm. Listen for the pick resting on the next string. Goal: identical attack on every note.

## 2. Accent pairs

One chord (a 6/9 shape), one bar per chord.

- Loud 1 and 3, soft 2 and 4.
- Loud 2 and 4, soft 1 and 3.
- Only 1 and 3 sound; 2 and 4 are silent strokes in the air.
- Only 2 and 4 sound.

Play each version for eight bars, then alternate without stopping. The hand motion must not change; only what sounds changes.

## 3. Silent bars

Comp eight bars, then play four bars of motion with no sound, then re-enter on the downbeat. This is the single best drill for internal time.

Variations: two silent bars in eight, one silent bar in four, silent bar on the last bar of each A section.

## 4. Metronome placement

Same 32 bars, four ways:

1. Click on every beat.
2. Click on 2 and 4 only.
3. Click on 1 only.
4. Click on the "and" of 4 only (hardest).

If the tempo wobbles when the click thins out, the time is being carried by the metronome, not the player.

## 5. One-chord exercise

Stay on a single 6/9 chord for 32 bars at 120, then 140, 160. No fills, no chord changes. This isolates the right hand from the left.

## 6. Two-chord exercise

I–IV or i–iv, one bar each, then two bars each. Listen for the top voice moving by step rather than jumping.

## 7. Tune comping, no solo

Comp a tune's form with no melody and no solo. Count the bars aloud. Stop at the top of each A section to prove you know where you are.

## 8. Comp behind a recording

Pick a recording with a clear rhythm guitar. Play along at reduced speed. Two passes:

- Pass 1: match the rhythm exactly.
- Pass 2: drop out for four bars every chorus and re-enter in time.

## 9. Duo with a bassist or a bass track

No drums. Comp for eight minutes. The bass owns 1 and 3; the guitar owns the choke. If there is no bassist, use a walking-bass recording or a metronome on 1 and 3.

## 10. Dynamics ladder

Four choruses of the same tune:

1. Almost silent, just time.
2. Medium, clear accents.
3. Full, driving.
4. Back to almost silent without changing the feel.

This is the drill that makes a student employable at a jam.

Rebuild the ladder whenever the choke starts to smear.
