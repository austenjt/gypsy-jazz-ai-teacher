# Curriculum

Four levels, loosely tied to what a player can do at a jam rather than to years of study. A student can sit in two levels at once; choose the level that matches their weakest essential skill, then work one item above it as a stretch.

Use these as scaffolds, not scripts. Adapt tempo targets and tune choices to the student's actual ceiling.

## Level 1 — Can hold the form

**Goal:** survive a slow jam and comp for someone else.

Milestones
- Three tunes from memory: changes and form, not just the head.
- La pompe at 120–140 bpm for a full 32-bar chorus without stopping.
- 6/9 and m6 shapes in the keys they actually play.
- Rest stroke on a two-octave major and minor arpeggio, 60–80 bpm.

Working material
- Rhythm: la pompe, one accent pattern, no fills.
- Harmony: I–vi–ii–V in C, F, Bb, G; minor i–iv–V in Am, Dm, Em.
- Soloing: arpeggios of the chords they comp, roots and 3rds only.
- Repertoire: two standards plus one minor-key tune.

Typical week
- 5 × 10 min la pompe with metronome.
- 4 × 10 min arpeggios, two keys.
- 3 × 15 min tune: form, then comping, then head.

## Level 2 — Can comp and follow

**Goal:** comp confidently behind a soloist at medium tempo and take a simple chorus.

Milestones
- La pompe at 160–180 bpm, with dynamics that respond to the soloist.
- Diminished passing chords and one substitution per tune, used deliberately.
- Solos built on guide tones and one vocabulary device (enclosure or arpeggio run).
- Five tunes learned cold, in jam keys.

Working material
- Rhythm: accent variation, two-bar comping phrases, stop-time.
- Harmony: tritone subs on V7, dim7 passing chords, minorization.
- Soloing: enclosures, chromatic approach, motif development, trading 4s.
- Repertoire: add three standards and one Django original.

Typical week
- 4 × 15 min la pompe, including 5 min behind a recording.
- 3 × 15 min vocabulary in two keys, then applied to one tune.
- 3 × 20 min new tune: learn the form, then the head, then comp it.

## Level 3 — Can solo over changes

**Goal:** play a coherent chorus that says something, at jam tempo.

Milestones
- Solos with a beginning, a middle, and an ending; rhythmically varied.
- Arpeggio and scale vocabulary connected across positions, not boxed.
- Reharmonization used as color, not avoidance.
- Eight to ten tunes, including a ballad and a waltz.
- Can transpose a phrase into any key on demand.

Working material
- Rhythm: comping in a duo, fills, intros and endings.
- Harmony: secondary dominants, backdoor, pedal points, diminished cycles.
- Soloing: transcription-driven vocabulary, long lines, tremolo, dynamics.
- Repertoire: Django repertoire, ballads, waltzes, bossa-tinged tunes.

Typical week
- 3 × 20 min transcription and transposition.
- 3 × 20 min solo construction on one tune, recorded.
- 2 × 20 min comping behind a recording, listening for the soloist's space.

## Level 4 — Can lead

**Goal:** lead a jam, teach, and sound like yourself.

Milestones
- Sets lists that work in real rooms: keys, tempos, transitions.
- Can teach any item above to a Level 1 player in ten minutes.
- Personal vocabulary: recognizable devices, not only transcribed ones.
- Comfortable at 220+ with clean articulation and no rushing.

Working material
- Arranging: intros, endings, modulations, medleys.
- Accompanying singers and horns.
- Recording and self-critique; comparing takes across weeks.
- Composition and repertoire curation.

## A 12-week block

A workable default when the student has 30–45 minutes a day:

| Weeks | Technique | Harmony | Repertoire |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Rest stroke, la pompe at 120–150 | 6/9 and m6 shapes in four keys | One tune, form + comp |
| 4–6 | Arpeggios across two positions | ii–V–I with one substitution | Second tune, head from memory |
| 7–9 | Enclosures, triplet bursts | Dim7 passing chords | Third tune, trade 4s |
| 10–12 | Tempo ladder to 200 | Turnarounds and endings | Play all three at tempo, recorded |

Adjust the tempo column to the student's ceiling. If they cannot comp cleanly at 120, the repertoire column pauses until they can.

## Progression checks

Every three weeks, have the student record one chorus of comping and one chorus of soloing. Compare against the previous recording on three axes only: time, tone, and ideas. Resist grading everything.
