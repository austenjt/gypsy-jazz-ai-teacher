# Practice sessions

A practice session is a container for one or two goals. Everything else is optional.

## Rules that make sessions work

- **Metronome first, music second.** Set the tempo before the hands move.
- **Slow is a tempo, not an apology.** If it breaks at 140, work at 120.
- **Two minutes per drill, then decide.** Extend only if it is improving.
- **Record once a week.** Thirty seconds of comping, thirty of soloing.
- **Stop while it still feels good.** Fatigue teaches sloppiness.

## 15-minute session

For busy days. One goal only.

```
2 min   Warm-up: rest-stroke open strings, then one arpeggio shape
8 min   The one thing: tempo ladder, 4 steps of 10 bpm
4 min   Apply it: play it inside a tune's first 8 bars
1 min   Write down the next tempo and what to fix
```

## 30-minute session

The default. Technique, harmony, repertoire.

```
5 min   Warm-up: la pompe at comfortable tempo, then arpeggios
10 min  Technique focus (see menu)
8 min   Harmony or vocabulary in one key, then transposed
5 min   Repertoire: one section, at tempo
2 min   Log: tempo reached, what broke
```

## 60-minute session

For days with real time. Split into two blocks with a short break.

```
10 min  Warm-up and tone: la pompe, rest stroke, one scale
15 min  Technique: the current bottleneck
15 min  Vocabulary: one device, three keys, then inside a tune
15 min  Repertoire: full tune, comp then solo, recorded
5 min   Listening: one chorus of a recording, with a specific question
```

## Drill menu

Pick one per session. Do not stack them.

**Right hand**
- Rest stroke: one string, downstrokes only, 60 bpm, listen for the click.
- String crossing: two adjacent strings, always downstroke when moving up in pitch.
- Tremolo: one note, 4 subdivisions, crescendo and decrescendo.

**La pompe**
- Metronome on 2 and 4 only; comp 32 bars without stopping.
- One chord, one bar each, whole-note changes, 120 → 180.
- Accent shift: accent 1 and 3, then 2 and 4, then only 2 and 4.
- Silent bar: drop one bar every eight and keep time internally.

**Harmony**
- One progression, four keys, 90 seconds each.
- Voice leading: move the smallest possible distance between chords.
- Substitution pass: same tune, one substitution per A section.

**Vocabulary**
- One 2-bar phrase, twelve keys, by ear.
- Enclosure drill: approach target from above and below, chromatically.
- Motif: play a 4-note idea, then three variations, then stop.

**Repertoire**
- Form only: play roots, count bars aloud.
- Head only: melody from memory, no chords.
- Comp only: behind a recording, matching the feel.
- Solo only: two choruses, one idea developed.

## Tempo ladders

Choose a start tempo where the drill is clean three times in a row.

| Step | Tempo | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | clean − 20 | No mistakes, relaxed |
| 2 | clean − 10 | No mistakes, still relaxed |
| 3 | clean | One pass, then repeat |
| 4 | clean + 10 | Two passes out of three |
| 5 | clean + 20 | Note where it breaks; stop |

Next session starts at step 3 of the previous ladder.

## Practice log

Keep it in a text file. One line per session.

```
date | drill | tempo | clean? | what broke | tomorrow
2026-11-17 | la pompe, metronome on 2&4 | 120-150 | yes | left hand tenses at 150 | 140, 8 bars silent
```

Review the log weekly. If a drill has not improved in three sessions, change the drill, not the tempo.
