# Tunes

A starter repertoire, not a canon. Keys are the ones commonly called at jams; they vary by region and by singer. Where a key or form is not certain, it says "varies" — check with the band rather than trusting a list.

Levels: **1** = can hold the form, **2** = can comp and follow, **3** = can solo over changes, **4** = can lead.

## Building a personal list

Keep a one-page list with columns: tune, key, form, tempo, status.

```
Tune            Key  Form            Tempo  Status
Minor Swing     Am   32 AABA         180    cold
All of Me       C    32              140    cold
Autumn Leaves   Gm   32              150    shaky bridge
Dark Eyes       Dm   16              120    melody only
Bossa Dorado    Dm   32              160    solo
```

Status vocabulary: *melody only*, *comping*, *solo*, *cold*, *performance ready*. Anything not marked *cold* is not learned.

## How to verify a key or a form

- Listen to two recordings and count bars together.
- Ask the person who called the tune. At a jam, "what key?" is not a rude question.
- Write down what the band actually played after the set, not what the chart said.
